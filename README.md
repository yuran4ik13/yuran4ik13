⭐ Featured project
💰 family_finance
All your accounts in one place, with a smart assistant that helps you spend better.

family_finance is a project I'm actively building, with the goal of turning it into a product that anyone who wants to track and control their finances can use.

🎯 The goal

People keep their money in many places: different banks, cards and crypto wallets. That makes it hard to answer a simple question: "Where does my money actually go?"

With family_finance, a user connects all their accounts once and gets one convenient dashboard with every expense, without switching between apps or filling in spreadsheets.

✅ Key features

🏦 Automatic transaction import from monobank and PrivatBank
🪙 Crypto transactions tracking: deposits, withdrawals and transfers in the same dashboard
📋 One unified list of transactions from all accounts, bank and crypto
🏷️ Spending grouped by categories (food, transport, subscriptions, …)
📊 Reports that show where the money goes each month
🔐 Bank API keys stored securely on the backend, never in the frontend
🤖 Coming next: AI financial assistant

I'm planning to integrate my own AI assistant / bot into the app. It will:

🔍 Analyze spending patterns and show where the money goes
💡 Find ways to optimize expenses, like unused subscriptions or categories that grow too fast
🧭 Give personal, practical advice on managing finances
The idea is simple: the app shouldn't just show numbers, it should help people make better decisions with their money.
